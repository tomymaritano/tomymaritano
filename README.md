# Hi, I'm Tomás Maritano

**I ship products.**

Lead Engineer at [Psynth](https://psynth.ai), based in Buenos Aires, Argentina. Started building
websites at twelve, studied at Universidad Tecnológica Nacional, and worked at Su Web Express,
Wolt, Grandvalira, Valere Realms, and Unicoin along the way. I started on frontend and ended up
owning the systems underneath it — turning messy inputs into documents people can actually act on.

## What I'm building

- **[Psynth](https://psynth.ai)** — clinical reporting that turns test results and intake into
  drafts psychologists review and sign.
- **Dripnex** — a hackable, AI-native note taker. Local SQLite, Markdown is export, an agentic
  chat that can search, read, and act on your own notes.
- **DolarGaucho** — quotes and a model that tracks the Argentine economic week, shipped as a
  website, a macOS menu bar app, and a desktop tray client.
- **Quantis-intel** — financial reporting for trading desks.
